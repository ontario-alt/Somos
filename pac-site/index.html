About the Committee

What is the Somos Group PAC?

The SOMOS Group PAC (“the Committee”) is a political committee organized under Internal Revenue Code section 527 and California Government Code section 82013(a) to support California state and local candidates and ballot measures.

The Committee does not make direct contributions to candidates. Instead, it contributes to other political action committees that support the candidates and issues the Committee supports — and makes certain that all of its activities supporting candidates and ballot measures comply with every legal requirement.

Contributor Fact Sheet

Frequently Asked Questions

Are there limits on how much I may contribute?

No. The Committee may accept contributions from individuals, corporations, other types of business entities, PACs, government contractors, lobbyists, and others, in any amount. It may not accept contributions from foreign nationals unless the individual is a permanent resident (green card holder). 52 U.S.C. § 30121

Because the Committee does not contribute directly to candidates, it may accept contributions in any amount from individuals and entities with pending real estate development projects anywhere in California.

Does contributing trigger SEC Rule 206(4)-5?

No. Because the Committee does not contribute directly to candidates and does not coordinate its activities with any candidate, contributions to the Committee do not trigger SEC Rule 206(4)-5, which prohibits certain financial advisors from making political contributions.

Federal Register at p. 41044, n.337 [PACs and political parties]; Federal Register at p. 41024 [independent expenditures]; see also Citizens United v. Fed. Election Comm’n (2010) 130 S. Ct. 876.

Are contributions tax deductible?

No. Contributions to the Committee are not tax deductible as charitable donations or business expenses. Int. Rev. Code §§ 162(e) & 6113(a)

Will my contribution be publicly disclosed?

Yes. The Committee is legally required to publicly disclose all contributions of $100 or more on reports (FPPC Form 460) filed with the Secretary of State, where the public can view them online. Cal. Gov. Code § 84211

Will I have to file my own campaign report?

Possibly. Individuals and entities that contribute $10,000 or more in a calendar year to all California state and local ballot measures, candidates, PACs, and political parties combined — including their contribution to the Committee — must file their own campaign reports (FPPC Form 461).

They must also file FPPC Form 497 within 24 hours of making a contribution of $1,000 or more to the Committee. Cal. Gov. Code §§ 82013(c), 84203 & 84211
